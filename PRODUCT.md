# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People writing and sketching personal notes in their own browser profile, often switching between quiet desktop writing and a narrow phone screen.

## Product Purpose

Blackboard Text is a local-first writing board. Text, drawings, portable backups and optional explicitly shared copies are the primary workflow. It requires no account and does not automatically upload or sync working notes.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, direct, unobtrusive. Existing theme choices and typography belong to the user.

## Anti-references

Persistent workspace headers, promotional chrome, account walls and controls covering the writing surface. Do not replace the existing settings design or disappearing scrollbar with a new visual language.

## Design Principles

- Keep the note dominant and secondary actions inside Settings.
- Drawing tools open beside page navigation only when requested. Opening any tool panel must never change note layout, stroke coordinates or scroll position.
- Improve input accuracy and data integrity before decorative changes.
- Preserve old notes, backup files and shared links.
- Show actionable save failures and accurate recovery state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, readable theme labels and reduced motion. Use comfortable touch targets and an optional description of drawings. Automated checks do not substitute for physical pen or screen-reader verification.
